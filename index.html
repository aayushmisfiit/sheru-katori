Your dog can't tell you he's unwell. His bowl can.

Dogs hide pain. The first sign is usually small: eating a little less, drinking a little more. Sheru catches those small signs for you, through every Indian season.

Garmi · April–June

Know he's drinking enough

When Delhi hits 44°C you'll get a message if he hasn't had enough paani, before the panting gets serious.

Simple science: a thirsty dog drinks less, not more, once dehydration sets in. Counting sips catches it.

Barsaat · July–Sept

Catch tick fever early

If he eats less for 2–3 days in a row, Sheru tells you to see the vet, while it's still a simple blood test.

Simple science: losing appetite is often the first sign of tick fever, days before a temperature.

Diwali · Oct–Nov

See how scared he really was

Find out which pataka nights he skipped dinner and how many days it took him to feel normal again.

Simple science: stressed dogs stop eating. The bowl shows fear you might not notice.

Ghar ka khana

Stop guessing portions

"Thoda sa dahi-chawal" gets weighed. You see exactly how much he ate, so the extra kilos don't sneak up.

Simple science: the base is a kitchen scale, accurate to 1 gram.

Mithai ka dabba

Keep the whole family in the loop

A festival checklist goes to everyone at home: no chocolate, no kaju katli with xylitol, no onion-garlic gravy.

Simple science: these foods are toxic to dogs even in small amounts.

Joint family

No more double dinners

Mummy fed him, Papa fed him, the didi next door fed him. Everyone sees one number in the app.

Simple science: every refill is weighed and logged with the time.

Science, seedhi baat

No jargon. Bas teen cheezein.

Just as you can tell when your mother isn't feeling well because she's skipped her chai, Sheru learns what normal looks like for your dog and notices when it changes.

१

It weighs

The bowl sits on a scale, like the one in your kitchen. It notes how much he eats and drinks, and when.

For you: an exact number, not a guess.

२

It learns his normal

In the first 2 weeks, Sheru figures out what a regular day looks like for your dog, not an average dog.

For you: alerts that fit him, not a textbook.

३

It tells you

When something drifts from normal for a few days, you get a WhatsApp-style message saying what changed and what to do.

For you: time to act, before it becomes an emergency.

Sheru is not a doctor. It never diagnoses. It tells you when to call one.
